# Wiki map

Fernbrook's team wiki. What lives where:

- runbooks/: short step lists. They assume you know the decisions and policies behind them.
- process/: how the team works: releases, on-call, reviews, planning.
- decisions/: why things are the way they are. Read these before you change how something works.
- architecture/: how the system is built and how a new version reaches users.
- policies/: rules that apply to every change.
- projects/, history/, team/: current projects, old notes, people.

Before you answer a question about a production change, read:

- decisions/why-we-deploy-this-way.md: when changes may go out
- architecture/system-architecture.md: how a new version reaches users
- policies/rollback-policy.md: when and how to revert
