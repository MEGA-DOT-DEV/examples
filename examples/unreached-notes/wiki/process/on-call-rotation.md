# On-call rotation

- Two people each week: a primary and a secondary. The rotation is in the team calendar.
- The primary is also the release captain for that week's trains.
- Handover is on Monday at 10:00, in a 15-minute call.
- Page the primary through #oncall. If there is no answer in 10 minutes, page the secondary.
- You get one free day after a week with a night page.
