# Reflection

Reflect when enough has happened to be worth it, and at least on the heartbeat.

1. Use `fs_read` on `events/` and `journal.jsonl`: compare what you wrote or sent with what the user did with it (the `outcome` events).
2. Weigh the signals. Linked, replied, or used: a strong yes. An edit: its diff is the lesson. Deleted: a strong no. Untouched: weak, and it fades; silence is unknown. Compare outputs with each other before you change how you work.
3. Use `fs_write` on `documents/attention-budget.md` only when the evidence supports a change, and name the events it rests on. Use grows a kind of output; neglect shrinks it. Keep the user's corrections.
4. Prune `documents/watches.md` and `documents/agenda.md`: drop what is done, impossible, or far from the user's focus. Threads close or retire; they do not pile up.
5. Set each next `look again` time from what you saw: little change means a later time. Respect the limits in `config.yaml`.
