# Attention budget

What I learned about the user's attention, from what they did with my work. Use grows a kind of output; neglect shrinks it.

- Notes: write one only for a source that meets the user's focus, and say how it connects to their notes.
- Digest: short, at most once a week until a reply or a link shows that more helps.
- Messages: only for one timely idea that cannot wait for the digest. None so far.

Silence is not approval, and it is not rejection either.
