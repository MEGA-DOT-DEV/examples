# Agenda

My open threads and intentions. Each item is a `##` title with three lines:

- why: why it matters to the user, in one sentence
- next: the next step
- look again: when to wake for it, as a date and time with its zone (for example 2026-11-06T09:00:00+01:00), or none

The harness wakes me at each `look again` time and writes the item's reason into the wake. Little change means a later time. I drop an item when it is done, impossible, or no longer near the user's focus; a question that waits for the user goes to documents/open-asks.md instead.

## Weekly digest
- why: A predictable rhythm: the user reads one short digest a week, and a week with nothing worth their time still gets one line.
- next: Gather what this week brought that meets their focus, and send one digest, or one line that nothing needs them.
- look again: none
