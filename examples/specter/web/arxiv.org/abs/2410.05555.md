# Exactly-once is a property of one store, not of a system

Synthetic lab content. This is not an actual arXiv paper.

Exactly-once delivery holds only inside one transactional store. Across two stores, such as an agent's log and an outside mail service, the best a system can give is at-least-once delivery with deduplication by key.

This contradicts designs that write the receipt after the effect and call the result exactly-once: a crash between the two writes leaves a duplicate that no local check can see.

The outside service must keep the key, or the deduplication does not exist.
